Where you start isn't fixed. Peffers et al. describe four legitimate entry points: problem-centred (start at Step 1), objective-centred (start at Step 2), design-centred — working from an existing artefact (start at Step 3), or client/context-initiated — starting from a solution already in use and working backward. Every route still passes through all six steps before it's done.
